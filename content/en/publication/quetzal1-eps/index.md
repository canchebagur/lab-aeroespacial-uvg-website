# Abstract
abstract: >
  <div style="text-align: justify;">
  QUETZAL-1, a 1U CubeSat developed by Universidad del Valle de Guatemala,
  flew from April to November 2020 with an in-house developed electrical power system (EPS).
  The EPS integrated six non-deployable solar panels with three MPPT chargers, a centralized
  architecture with +3.3 VDC, +5 VDC, and +7.6 VDC rails powered by an unregulated main bus
  connected to a +4.2 VDC rechargeable Li-ion battery, along with protections, a battery heater,
  and deployment switches. The article describes the design and on-orbit performance, showing a
  positive power budget throughout the mission, even during high beta angle seasons when panel
  efficiency decreases, and how the heater prevented battery freezing during long eclipses.
  </div>
