<div style="text-align: justify;">

Andrés Viau N. is a Mechanical Engineer and researcher at the Aerospace Laboratory of Universidad del Valle de Guatemala (UVG), where he participates in research projects related to the design and development of space technology. He has extensive experience in mechanical component design using CAD software, computer-aided manufacturing (CAM) process programming, and applied engineering project management.
<br></br>
With over 14 years of teaching experience in the field of mechanical engineering, he has been involved in projects ranging from the development of components for aerospace applications to the optimization of processes and products in the agricultural sector. His work combines engineering, innovation, and education to advance technical and scientific training in Guatemala.
