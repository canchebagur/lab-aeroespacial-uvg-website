Kuk Ho Chung is a Mechatronics Engineer and advisor at the Aerospace Laboratory of Universidad del Valle de Guatemala (UVG), where he supports research projects focused on the development of space technology. His experience spans embedded systems, nanosatellites, high-performance propulsion systems, Internet of Things (IoT), artificial intelligence (AI), as well as technical documentation applied to industrial ecosystems.

He combines knowledge in aerospace, automotive, mechanical, and electronic engineering for system optimization through AI. His work integrates power architectures, multilevel embedded computing, and machine learning, with a focus on technical precision, rigorous documentation, and effective knowledge transfer.

Passionate about technological innovation, he drives the convergence between engineering and artificial intelligence to create efficient and scalable solutions that contribute to Guatemala's scientific and technological advancement.
